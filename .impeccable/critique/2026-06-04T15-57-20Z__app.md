## Anti-Patterns Verdict

Two AI tells survive: (1) eyebrow label on every panel header ("Library", "Status", "Topology") — the absolute-ban pattern; (2) theatrical tech-readout copy in NowPlaying ("ACTIVE_DATA_STREAM", "Registry ID", "Signal Status", "Registry Search") — AI performing the instrument aesthetic rather than embodying it.

## Overall Impression

The underlying system is genuinely well-designed and technically impressive. What undermines it is the copy layer, which performs the theme instead of embodying it. Remove the theatrical labels and the section eyebrows, and the interface steps from "well-executed AI aesthetic" into "confident, self-assured instrument."

## What's Working

1. **Reactive palette system** — forced-dark constraint, live CSS variable injection, bass-reactive border at 60fps. Technically sophisticated and visually distinctive.
2. **Physical control language** — 2px radius + spring mechanics + inset highlight. Reads exactly like hardware.
3. **Keyboard shortcut depth** — Space, /, J/K, F, V. Well-implemented; problem is purely discoverability.
